The Game is designed to be playable without creating a player account or signing in. It is not intended to require a phone number, email login, or social login to play.

If you choose to share WildCatapult, the Game may open your device's Android sharing interface with a prepared message about the Game. You decide whether to select a sharing app and send the message.

Your device operating system, app store, and other platform providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their processing is separate from the Game's own practices.

The Game may be accessible to younger players. Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy may contact us using the email address provided in this policy.
